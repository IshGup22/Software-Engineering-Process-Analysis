# Agile User Stories

The following user stories translate business and functional requirements into delivery-ready Agile requirements.

## US-01 — Submit Change Request

**As a business requester,**  
I want to submit a standardized software change request  
**so that** the delivery team receives the information required to understand my business need.

**Priority:** High

**Related Requirements:** BR-01, FR-01

---

## US-02 — Validate Request Completeness

**As a business analyst,**  
I want incomplete requests to be identified before engineering review  
**so that** engineering teams do not spend time assessing unclear requirements.

**Priority:** High

**Related Requirements:** BR-02, FR-02

---

## US-03 — Track Request Status

**As a stakeholder,**  
I want to view the current status and owner of a request  
**so that** I do not need to rely on manual email follow-ups for progress updates.

**Priority:** High

**Related Requirements:** BR-03, BR-04, FR-03, FR-04

---

## US-04 — Review Technical Feasibility

**As an engineer,**  
I want to record feasibility, dependencies, risks, and clarification needs  
**so that** implementation decisions are based on documented technical analysis.

**Priority:** High

**Related Requirements:** BR-06, FR-06

---

## US-05 — Prioritize Approved Work

**As a product owner,**  
I want approved requests converted into prioritized backlog items  
**so that** the team can plan work according to business value and delivery capacity.

**Priority:** High

**Related Requirements:** BR-07, FR-07

---

## US-06 — Define Acceptance Criteria

**As a developer,**  
I want each user story to contain clear acceptance criteria before development begins  
**so that** expected behaviour is understood before implementation.

**Priority:** High

**Related Requirements:** BR-08, FR-08

---

## US-07 — Trace Requirements to Testing

**As a QA analyst,**  
I want test scenarios linked to requirements and acceptance criteria  
**so that** I can verify that the delivered change meets the documented business need.

**Priority:** High

**Related Requirements:** BR-05, BR-09, FR-05, FR-09

---

## US-08 — Record Test Results

**As a QA analyst,**  
I want to record test outcomes and associated defects  
**so that** unresolved issues are visible before business acceptance.

**Priority:** Medium

**Related Requirements:** BR-09, FR-09

---

## US-09 — Approve Delivered Change

**As a business approver,**  
I want to review and approve successfully tested functionality  
**so that** only changes meeting the intended business need are marked ready for release.

**Priority:** High

**Related Requirements:** BR-10, FR-10

---

## US-10 — Review Change History

**As a business analyst,**  
I want to review the status, ownership, and approval history of a request  
**so that** decisions and handoffs remain traceable throughout delivery.

**Priority:** Medium

**Related Requirements:** BR-05, BR-11, FR-11

---

## US-11 — Prevent Premature Progression

**As a process owner,**  
I want workflow rules to prevent incomplete requests from progressing  
**so that** required reviews, testing, and approvals are completed in the correct sequence.

**Priority:** High

**Related Requirements:** BR-12, FR-12
